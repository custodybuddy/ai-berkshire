# Comment Mining Workflow

## Goal

Turn public comments into a voice-of-customer report containing recurring
questions, objections, needs, language, and content opportunities.

## Inputs

- Post, video, or account URLs
- Brand and audience context
- Optional themes to investigate
- Collection window and sample size

## Procedure

1. Define the sample and record URLs, platform, post date, and collection date.
2. Remove duplicates, obvious spam, automated replies, and irrelevant comments.
3. Preserve short verbatim excerpts only when they are useful evidence; avoid
   collecting unnecessary personal information.
4. Classify comments into questions, pain points, objections, desired outcomes,
   product language, testimonials, misinformation, and buying signals.
5. Count themes carefully and state whether counts are exhaustive or sampled.
6. Separate audience statements from the analyst's interpretation.
7. Convert validated themes into content angles, FAQs, experiments, or product
   research questions.

## Output

```markdown
# Voice-of-Customer Report
## Scope and sampling method
## Top themes
## Audience questions
## Pain points and objections
## Exact audience language
## Content opportunities
## Limitations and confidence
```

Do not present comments as representative of the whole audience without a
sampling caveat.
