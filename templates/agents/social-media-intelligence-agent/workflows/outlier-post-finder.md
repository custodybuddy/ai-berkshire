# Outlier Post Finder Workflow

## Goal

Find content that performs unusually well relative to the same creator's own
normal output, then explain the pattern without treating correlation as proof.

## Inputs

- Creator or account URLs
- Platform and content type
- Sampling window and desired number of posts
- Available public metrics

## Procedure

1. Establish the sampling frame: account, platform, date range, and content
   type.
2. Collect a consistent sample of posts and record URL, date, format, topic,
   hook, caption, visible engagement, and any available view data.
3. Prefer a creator-relative baseline such as median views or engagement for
   comparable posts. If the baseline cannot be calculated, say so.
4. Flag candidate outliers using a transparent rule, such as a multiple of the
   creator median or a clearly unusual engagement rate.
5. Inspect the strongest candidates for hook, promise, tension, proof,
   emotional trigger, structure, length, visual treatment, and call to action.
6. Distinguish observed patterns from hypotheses that require testing.
7. Produce test ideas adapted to the brand; do not recommend copying a
   creator's identity, wording, or protected creative expression.

## Output

```markdown
# Outlier Content Report
## Scope and baseline method
## Outlier table
## Repeated patterns
## Likely explanations
## Brand-safe test ideas
## Caveats and source links
```

Never rank posts by raw views alone when a meaningful creator-relative baseline
is available.
