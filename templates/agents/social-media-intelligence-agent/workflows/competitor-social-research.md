# Competitor Social Research Workflow

## Goal

Compare competitors or adjacent creators on public social content and identify
defensible opportunities for the brand.

## Inputs

- Brand brief from `prompts/brand-intake.md`
- Competitor or creator list
- Platforms and time window
- Optional priority products, audiences, or topics

## Procedure

1. Define the comparison set and explain why each account belongs in it.
2. Apply the same observation fields to every account: pillars, formats,
   posting cadence, hooks, proof, offers, CTAs, audience response, and visible
   engagement signals.
3. Compare content themes and formats, not just follower totals.
4. Identify repeated category conventions, whitespace, underserved questions,
   and opportunities where the brand has a credible right to participate.
5. Separate direct competitors from attention competitors and inspiration
   accounts.
6. Recommend experiments with a hypothesis, target audience, format, metric,
   and stop condition.

## Output

```markdown
# Competitor Social Intelligence Brief
## Scope and comparison set
## Account snapshots
## Comparison matrix
## Common category patterns
## Content gaps and opportunities
## Recommended experiments
## Evidence gaps and source links
```

Avoid claims such as "best" or "most effective" unless the evidence and
comparison method support them.
