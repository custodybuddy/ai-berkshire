# CustodyBuddy Example

## Request

Analyze public family-law educators and divorce creators. Find common questions,
emotional pain points, educational gaps, and safe content opportunities.

## Suggested workflow

1. `creator-research.md`
2. `comment-mining.md`
3. `competitor-social-research.md`

## Expected deliverable

Content strategy brief that distinguishes observed public language from legal or
clinical interpretation. Avoid individualized legal advice, private-person
profiling, and unsupported claims about safety or outcomes.
