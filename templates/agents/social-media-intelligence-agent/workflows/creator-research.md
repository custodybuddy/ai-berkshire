# Creator Research Workflow

## Goal

Build a useful, evidence-backed map of creators, educators, publishers, or
brand accounts relevant to a specific audience or market.

## Inputs

- Brand brief from `prompts/brand-intake.md`
- Research request from `prompts/research-request.md`
- Target topic, geography, platforms, and time window
- Candidate accounts or a discovery brief

## Procedure

1. Define relevance before searching: audience overlap, topic authority,
   platform fit, geography, and commercial adjacency.
2. Separate discovery candidates from verified candidates.
3. Record the account URL, platform, account name, niche, audience clues,
   posting formats, visible engagement signals, and collection date.
4. Use representative samples rather than judging an account from one post.
5. Identify recurring content pillars, formats, hooks, offers, and calls to
   action.
6. Assign confidence to audience and performance inferences.
7. Pass strong candidates to `outlier-post-finder.md` and
   `competitor-social-research.md` when appropriate.

## Output

```markdown
# Creator Research Brief
## Scope and data cutoff
## Shortlist table
## Why each account is relevant
## Recurring content patterns
## Evidence gaps and confidence
## Recommended accounts for deeper research
```

Do not equate follower count with influence, audience fit, expertise, or
commercial value.
