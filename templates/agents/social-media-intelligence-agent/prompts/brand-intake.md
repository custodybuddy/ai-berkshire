# Brand Intake Prompt

Use this prompt before a brand-specific research task:

```text
Brand:
Approved one-sentence description:
Primary audience:
Audience geography:
Products or offers:
Priority problems or topics:
Social channels:
Known brand accounts:
Competitors or adjacent accounts:
Brand voice:
Claims or topics to avoid:
Research period:
Desired deliverable:
```

If a field is unknown, write `unknown`. Do not fill it with an assumption.
