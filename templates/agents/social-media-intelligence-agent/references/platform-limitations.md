# Platform Limitations

- Public metrics can be incomplete, delayed, hidden, or displayed differently
  across platforms.
- Platform algorithms and ranking systems are not directly observable from a
  post sample.
- Comment samples can be biased toward highly motivated or dissatisfied users.
- Follower counts do not establish audience fit or purchasing intent.
- Deleted, private, logged-in-only, or paywalled material is out of scope unless
  the user supplies it lawfully and directly.
- Current data must be dated. Do not call a result "latest" without a current
  collection date.

When access is unavailable, provide a research plan or analyze user-supplied
links instead of inventing results.
