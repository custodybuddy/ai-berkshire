# Source Quality and Evidence Rules

## Preferred evidence

1. Direct public post, video, profile, ad, or comment URL
2. Official brand or creator statement
3. Reputable secondary reporting that links to primary evidence
4. Search snippets or aggregators only as discovery leads

## Required labeling

- **Observed:** directly visible in the source.
- **Calculated:** derived from recorded observations using a stated method.
- **Interpreted:** a reasoned explanation that may be wrong.
- **Recommended:** a proposed action or experiment.

## Confidence

- **High:** repeated, directly sourced, and consistently observed.
- **Medium:** supported by several signals but limited by sample or access.
- **Low:** plausible interpretation based on thin or indirect evidence.

Do not describe public social data as complete, representative, or causal unless
the collection method supports that claim.
